# Resume Section Builder

## When to Use This Skill

Use when user needs help with specific resume sections or is unsure what to include. Mentions: "resume sections", "skills section", "summary section", "what to include".

## Professional Summary by Career Stage

**Entry Level:** Recent [degree] graduate with [internship/project] experience. Skilled in [skills]. Seeking to [goal].
**Mid-Career:** [Title] with [X] years [domain]. Track record of [key result]. Expert in [top 3 skills].
**Executive:** [Function] executive with [X]+ years [domain]. Proven success in [transformations]. P&L ownership of [scale].
**Career Changer:** [Target role] professional with [X] years [transferable skill]. Background in [old field] provides unique perspective on [relevant aspect].

## Skills Section Format

Simple list (ATS-optimized) or Categorized (Languages / Frameworks / Databases / Cloud / Tools)

**Include:** Technical tools, methodologies, certifications, domain knowledge
**Exclude:** Microsoft Office, "basic" skills, skills you can't discuss in interview

## Section Order by Role

**Technical roles:** Contact → Skills → Experience → Projects → Education
**Recent grad:** Contact → Education → Skills → Experience → Projects
**Executive:** Contact → Executive Summary → Career Highlights → Experience → Board Roles → Education
**Career changer:** Contact → Summary (explaining transition) → Skills → Experience (reframed) → Bridge Experience → Education

## Additional Sections (When to Add)

- **Projects:** Entry level, career changers, technical roles with personal work
- **Volunteer:** Relevant leadership, fills gaps meaningfully
- **Languages:** Only if relevant to role; include proficiency levels
- **Publications:** Academic, research, or thought leadership roles
