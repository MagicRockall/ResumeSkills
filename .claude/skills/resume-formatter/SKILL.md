# Resume Formatter

## When to Use This Skill

Use when user needs help with resume layout, has a messy resume, or wants ATS compatibility. Mentions: "format resume", "resume layout", "clean resume".

## Document Setup

- **Length:** Entry level: 1 page | Mid: 1-2 pages | Senior/Exec: 2 pages
- **Margins:** 0.5"-1" all sides
- **Fonts:** Arial, Calibri, Georgia, Times New Roman (10-12pt body, 14-16pt headers)
- **Line spacing:** 1.0-1.15

## ATS-Safe Rules

**DO:** Single column, standard fonts, simple bullets (•, -, *), standard section headers, contact info in body, save as .docx or text-based .pdf

**DON'T:** Tables, text boxes, columns, headers/footers, images/graphics, unusual fonts, skill bars/ratings, special characters

## File Naming

`FirstName_LastName_Resume.pdf` or `FirstName_LastName_Resume_RoleName.pdf`

## Quick Formatting Checklist

- ✅ 1-2 pages
- ✅ Standard font 10-12pt body
- ✅ Consistent formatting throughout
- ✅ Clear section headers
- ✅ No tables, text boxes, or columns
- ✅ Contact info in body (not header)
- ✅ Saved as .docx or .pdf
- ✅ Proper file name
