# Career Changer Translator

## When to Use This Skill

Use when user is switching industries or careers. Mentions: "career change", "transferable skills", "pivot", "new industry".

## Career Change Strategy

1. Lead with transferable skills, not job titles
2. Translate all experience into target industry language
3. Build bridge experiences (volunteer, freelance, certs)
4. Tell a compelling "why" story

## Universal Transferable Skills

Leadership, Project Management, Communication, Data Analysis, Problem-Solving, Stakeholder Management, Budget Oversight

## Common Translations

| Teaching → Corporate | Military → Corporate | Retail → Sales |
|---------------------|---------------------|----------------|
| Lesson plans → Curriculum | Mission → Project | Customers → Accounts |
| Students → Learners | Unit → Team | Upselling → Cross-selling |
| Classroom mgmt → Facilitation | Briefing → Presentation | Store sales → Revenue gen |

## "Why Are You Changing?" Story Framework

1. **Discovery:** "Through [experience], I discovered my passion for [new field]..."
2. **Connection:** "My experience in [old field] gives me unique perspective because..."
3. **Action:** "I've prepared by [certifications, projects, volunteering]..."
4. **Vision:** "I'm excited to bring [skill] to [company] because..."
