# Resume Bullet Writer

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when the user wants to:
- Write or improve resume bullet points
- Transform weak descriptions into strong achievements
- Add metrics and quantifiable results
- Mentions: "improve my bullets", "make my resume stronger", "quantify my achievements"

## Core Frameworks

### The X-Y-Z Formula (Google Method)
**Structure:** "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]"

### Power Verbs by Category

**Leadership:** Led, Directed, Managed, Spearheaded, Orchestrated
**Achievement:** Achieved, Delivered, Exceeded, Secured, Won
**Growth:** Grew, Increased, Boosted, Expanded, Scaled
**Creation:** Created, Developed, Designed, Built, Launched
**Optimization:** Streamlined, Optimized, Enhanced, Automated

## Bullet Strength Checklist

Every bullet should have:
- ✅ Strong action verb (avoid "responsible for", "helped")
- ✅ At least one number/metric
- ✅ Specific outcome or result
- ✅ Context of scale (team size, budget, users, etc.)
- ✅ 1-2 lines maximum
- ✅ Reads as an achievement, not a duty

## Transformation Examples

❌ WEAK: "Managed social media accounts"
✅ STRONG: "Grew Instagram following by 260% (5K to 18K) through content strategy and influencer partnerships, generating 200+ leads"

❌ WEAK: "Responsible for managing team"
✅ STRONG: "Led cross-functional team of 12 to deliver $2M product, increasing revenue by 35%"

## Quantification Strategies

**Types of Metrics:**
- Money: revenue generated, costs saved, budget managed
- Percentages: growth rates, improvement %, efficiency gains
- Time: hours saved, cycle time reduced
- Volume: customers served, projects managed, team size
- Quality: satisfaction scores, error rates, SLA adherence
