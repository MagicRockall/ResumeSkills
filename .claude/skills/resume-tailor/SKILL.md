# Resume Tailor

## When to Use This Skill

Use when user wants to customize their resume for a specific job. Use AFTER job-description-analyzer.

## Tailoring Process

1. Rewrite Professional Summary to mirror job requirements
2. Reorder Skills section by relevance
3. Reorder experience bullets to lead with most relevant
4. Add job description keywords naturally
5. Adjust language to match industry terminology

## Truth vs Tailoring

**Acceptable:** Reordering, emphasizing relevant experience, matching terminology
**NOT acceptable:** Adding skills you don't have, changing metrics, fabricating experience

## Quick Checklist

- ✅ Summary mentions exact job title/function
- ✅ Top skills match job's top requirements
- ✅ Most relevant experience positioned first
- ✅ Keywords from JD appear naturally
- ✅ All claims are truthful
- ✅ ATS formatting maintained
