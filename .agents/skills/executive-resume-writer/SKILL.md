# Executive Resume Writer

## When to Use This Skill

Use when user is applying for C-suite, VP, or Director roles (15+ years experience). Mentions: "executive resume", "C-suite", "VP resume", "senior leadership".

## Executive Resume Structure

1. Executive Profile/Summary
2. Core Competencies (Leadership Themes)
3. Career Highlights / Key Achievements
4. Professional Experience
5. Board & Advisory Roles
6. Education & Executive Development

## Key Differences from Standard Resume

- **Focus:** Strategic impact and transformation, not task execution
- **Length:** 2-3 pages acceptable
- **Metrics:** P&L size, org scale, revenue growth, M&A activity
- **Language:** "Spearheaded", "Orchestrated", "Championed" — not "Managed", "Helped"

## Experience Format

Always include: Company context (revenue, employees, stage), reporting structure, P&L/budget responsibility, strategic scope.

## Career Highlights Section

Place 4-6 biggest achievements upfront:
- Led transformation from $X to $Y revenue
- Executed N acquisitions totaling $XM
- Built global team from X to Y employees
- Prepared company for IPO/exit
