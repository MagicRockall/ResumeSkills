# Interview Prep Generator

## When to Use This Skill

Use when user wants to prepare for an interview, practice questions, or create STAR stories. Mentions: "interview prep", "STAR stories", "behavioral questions".

## STAR Method

- **S**ituation: Set context (1-2 sentences)
- **T**ask: Your responsibility (1 sentence)
- **A**ction: What YOU did specifically (2-3 sentences)
- **R**esult: Quantified outcome (1-2 sentences)

## Story Bank Categories

Build stories for: Leadership, Problem-Solving, Collaboration, Achievement, Failure/Growth

## Common Question Types

**Behavioral:** "Tell me about a time you led a team / solved a complex problem / failed..."
**Role-specific:** Prioritization, metrics, technical challenges
**Standard:** "Tell me about yourself", "Why this role?", "Why this company?"

## Output Format

Provide: Role Analysis (competencies they'll test), Predicted Questions mapped to stories, STAR Story Bank (8-10 stories), "Tell Me About Yourself" script, Questions to Ask, Company Research Notes.

## Questions to Avoid Asking

- ❌ Salary/benefits (save for HR)
- ❌ Questions you can Google
- ❌ Yes/no questions
