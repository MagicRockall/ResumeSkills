# Portfolio Case Study Writer

## When to Use This Skill

Use when user wants to create portfolio case studies, expand resume bullets into detailed writeups. Mentions: "case study", "portfolio", "project writeup".

## Case Study Structure

1. **Overview** — project summary, role, timeline, team, one-line impact
2. **Problem** — business context, user pain points, key metrics/goals, constraints
3. **Process** — research conducted, hypotheses, decisions made (and why)
4. **Solution** — what you built/created with visuals described
5. **Results** — quantified impact with before/after table
6. **Learnings** — what worked, what you'd do differently

## Quality Checklist

- ✅ Clear problem statement
- ✅ Evidence of user/customer focus
- ✅ Process clearly explained
- ✅ YOUR specific contributions are clear (not just team)
- ✅ Quantified results
- ✅ Visual artifacts described/included
- ✅ Honest about challenges
- ✅ 3-10 minute read
- ✅ Proofread and polished
