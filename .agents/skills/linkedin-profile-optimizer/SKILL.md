# LinkedIn Profile Optimizer

## When to Use This Skill

Use when user wants to optimize LinkedIn for job searching, improve visibility, or attract recruiters.

## Key Sections to Optimize

### Headline (220 chars max)
Formula: [Role] | [Key Expertise] | [Value Proposition]
Example: "Senior Product Manager | B2B SaaS | Driving 0→1 Products to $10M ARR"

### About Section (1500-2000 chars recommended)
Structure: Hook → Who you are → Key achievements → What you're seeking → Skills keywords → CTA

### Experience
- More detail than resume (no page limit)
- Include media/links
- 4-6 achievement bullets per role

### Skills
- List all 50 available skills
- Order by relevance
- Get endorsements for top 3

## All-Star Profile Checklist

- ✅ Professional photo
- ✅ Custom headline
- ✅ Current position with description
- ✅ Two past positions
- ✅ Education
- ✅ 5+ skills
- ✅ 50+ connections
- ✅ Featured section populated
- ✅ 5+ recommendations
