# Academic CV Builder

## When to Use This Skill

Use when user is applying for academic positions (faculty, postdoc, research). Mentions: "academic CV", "curriculum vitae", "faculty position", "professor resume".

## Academic CV vs Resume

| Resume | Academic CV |
|--------|------------|
| 1-2 pages | 2-20+ pages |
| Results-focused | Scholarship-focused |
| Skills section prominent | Publications prominent |

## Standard Section Order

1. Contact Information (include ORCID)
2. Education (with dissertation title, advisor, committee)
3. Academic Appointments
4. Publications (peer-reviewed, books, under review)
5. Presentations (invited talks, conferences)
6. Grants & Funding (with amounts and your role: PI/Co-PI)
7. Teaching Experience
8. Mentoring
9. Service (to profession, university, department)
10. Professional Memberships
11. Honors & Awards

## Publications Formatting

- **Bold your name** in author lists
- Include DOIs
- Separate by category (articles, book chapters, under review, in preparation)
- Number in reverse chronological order (sciences)

## CV Length by Career Stage

Graduate Student: 2-4 pages | Postdoc: 3-6 | Early Faculty: 5-10 | Senior Faculty: 15-30+

# Cover Letter Generator

## When to Use This Skill

Use when user wants to write a cover letter. Use AFTER analyzing the job description.

## Structure (250-400 words, 3-4 paragraphs)

1. **Opening Hook** — specific company knowledge, mutual connection, or impressive achievement. Never start with "I am writing to apply..."
2. **Body 1** — your strongest relevant experience + specific metric
3. **Body 2** — additional value or address gaps
4. **Closing** — specific enthusiasm + call to action

## Opening Hook Types

- Company Knowledge: "I noticed [Company]'s recent [initiative]..."
- Achievement-Led: "Last year I [impressive result]..."
- Problem-Solver: "Your JD mentions [challenge] — I've navigated this exact situation..."
- Referral: "[Name] on your team suggested I reach out..."

## Quality Checklist

- ✅ Opens with a hook (not "I am writing to apply")
- ✅ Mentions specific company knowledge
- ✅ Connects experience to job requirements
- ✅ Includes at least one metric
- ✅ Addresses gaps if applicable
- ✅ 250-400 words
- ✅ Clear call to action

- **S**ituation: Set context (1-2 sentences)

## When to Use This Skill

- 90-100%: Overqualified
- 75-89%: Excellent fit — apply immediately

# Offer Comparison Analyzer

## When to Use This Skill

Use when user has multiple offers to compare. Mentions: "compare offers", "multiple offers", "which job", "deciding between jobs".

## Total Compensation Formula

**Total Comp = Base + Bonus + Equity (annual) + 401k Match + Benefits Value + Perks Value**

Always calculate Year 1 (includes signing bonus) AND ongoing annually.

## Weighted Decision Matrix

Have user rank these factors by importance:
- Total Compensation (suggested 25%)
- Career Growth (suggested 25%)
- Work-Life Balance (suggested 20%)
- Team & Culture (suggested 20%)
- Location/Commute (suggested 10%)

Score each offer 1-10 per factor, multiply by weight, sum for final score.

## Red Flags to Watch

- Vague bonus language ("up to X%")
- Equity with no liquidity path
- Verbal promises not in writing
- High turnover (check LinkedIn tenure)
- Recent layoffs/reorgs
- Non-compete restrictions

## The Gut Check Questions

- Which would I regret NOT taking?
- Which aligns with my 5-year goals?
- Which team do I want to work with Monday morning?
- Where will I grow more in 3 years?

# Resume ATS Optimizer

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when the user wants to:
- Optimize their resume for Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)
- Check if their resume will pass automated screening
- Understand why their applications aren't getting responses
- Mentions keywords like: "ATS", "not getting interviews", "resume not working", "optimize resume", "keyword optimization"

Also use when the user provides a resume file and mentions they're applying to jobs.

## Core Capabilities

- Parse resume and test ATS compatibility
- Extract and analyze keywords against job descriptions
- Identify formatting issues that break ATS parsers
- Calculate match scores between resume and job postings
- Suggest keyword additions and placements
- Generate ATS-friendly formatting recommendations

## The ATS Problem

75% of resumes are rejected by Applicant Tracking Systems before a human ever sees them. Companies use ATS to:
- Filter out unqualified candidates automatically
- Search for specific keywords from job requirements
- Parse resumes into structured data
- Rank candidates by keyword match percentage

Common reasons resumes fail ATS:
1. Poor formatting (tables, columns, headers/footers)
2. Missing keywords from job description
3. Inconsistent section headers
4. Non-standard fonts or special characters
5. Text embedded in images
6. Incorrect file format

## ATS Compatibility Checklist

### File Format
- ✅ Use .docx or .pdf (not .pages, .odt)
- ✅ PDF must be text-based, not scanned image
- ✅ File name: "FirstName_LastName_Resume.pdf"

### Font & Formatting
- ✅ Standard fonts: Arial, Calibri, Georgia, Times New Roman
- ✅ Font size: 10-12pt for body, 14-16pt for headers
- ✅ No text boxes, tables, or columns
- ✅ No headers/footers (put contact info in body)
- ✅ No images, graphics, or charts
- ✅ Consistent date formats (MM/YYYY)
- ✅ Standard bullet points (•, -, *)

### Section Headers
Use standard, recognizable headers:
- ✅ "Professional Experience" or "Work Experience" (not "Where I've Been")
- ✅ "Education" (not "Academic Background")
- ✅ "Skills" (not "Core Competencies")
- ✅ "Summary" or "Professional Summary"

### Contact Information
```
John Smith
email@example.com | (555) 123-4567 | LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/johnsmith
San Francisco, CA
```

NOT in header/footer, and avoid:
- ❌ Tables for contact info
- ❌ Special characters in email
- ❌ Multiple phone numbers
- ❌ Full mailing address (city/state is enough)

## Keyword Optimization Process

### Step 1: Extract Job Description Keywords

Identify three types of keywords:

**Hard Skills (Technical)**
- Programming languages (Python, Java, SQL)
- Tools and platforms (Salesforce, AWS, Excel)
- Certifications (PMP, CPA, CFA)
- Methodologies (Agile, Six Sigma, SDLC)

**Soft Skills**
- Leadership, collaboration, communication
- Problem-solving, analytical thinking
- Project management, stakeholder management

**Industry Terms**
- B2B, SaaS, e-commerce
- Enterprise, SMB, mid-market
- ARR, MRR, churn rate

### Step 2: Match Analysis

For each keyword in job description:
1. Check if exact phrase appears in resume
2. Check for synonyms or variations
3. Count frequency of mention
4. Note location (summary, experience, skills)

### Step 3: Calculate Match Score

```
Match Score = (Keywords Matched / Total Required Keywords) × 100

Target: 80%+ for strong match
```

### Step 4: Keyword Placement Strategy

**Priority 1: Professional Summary** - Include 5-8 most important keywords
**Priority 2: Skills Section** - List keywords explicitly
**Priority 3: Experience Bullets** - Incorporate keywords into achievement statements

## Implementation Checklist

1. ✅ Scan current resume for ATS compatibility issues
2. ✅ Analyze job description for required keywords
3. ✅ Calculate current match score
4. ✅ Identify specific missing keywords
5. ✅ Suggest exact placements for new keywords
6. ✅ Flag formatting problems
7. ✅ Provide before/after examples
8. ✅ Re-score after suggested changes

# Resume Bullet Writer

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when the user wants to:
- Write or improve resume bullet points
- Transform weak descriptions into strong achievements
- Add metrics and quantifiable results
- Mentions: "improve my bullets", "make my resume stronger", "quantify my achievements"

## Core Frameworks

### The X-Y-Z Formula (Google Method)
**Structure:** "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]"

### Power Verbs by Category

**Leadership:** Led, Directed, Managed, Spearheaded, Orchestrated
**Achievement:** Achieved, Delivered, Exceeded, Secured, Won
**Growth:** Grew, Increased, Boosted, Expanded, Scaled
**Creation:** Created, Developed, Designed, Built, Launched

Every bullet should have:
- ✅ Strong action verb (avoid "responsible for", "helped")
- ✅ At least one number/metric
- ✅ Specific outcome or result
- ✅ Context of scale (team size, budget, users, etc.)
- ✅ 1-2 lines maximum
- ✅ Reads as an achievement, not a duty

## Transformation Examples

❌ WEAK: "Managed social media accounts"
✅ STRONG: "Grew Instagram following by 260% (5K to 18K) through content strategy and influencer partnerships, generating 200+ leads"

❌ WEAK: "Responsible for managing team"
✅ STRONG: "Led cross-functional team of 12 to deliver $2M product, increasing revenue by 35%"

## Quantification Strategies

**Types of Metrics:**
- Money: revenue generated, costs saved, budget managed
- Percentages: growth rates, improvement %, efficiency gains
- Time: hours saved, cycle time reduced
- Volume: customers served, projects managed, team size
- Quality: satisfaction scores, error rates, SLA adherence

# Resume Quantifier

## When to Use This Skill

Use when user needs to add metrics, says they "don't have numbers", or mentions: "add metrics", "quantify", "no data".

## Key Principle

**Every bullet can be quantified.** If you think your work can't be measured, you haven't asked the right questions yet.

## Discovery Questions

- How many people/projects/customers?
- What was the budget/revenue involved?
- What changed because of your work?
- How was it before vs. after?
- How did you compare to peers/benchmarks?

## Estimation Techniques

**Conservative estimate:** If you think 60%, say 50%. Better to understate.
**Use ranges:** "team of 8-12", "$100K-$150K in revenue"
**Use minimums:** "100+ customers", "at least 15 projects"
**Calculate from frequency:** 5 clients/week × 50 weeks = "250+ clients annually"

## Metric Categories

- **Money:** Revenue generated, costs saved, budget managed
- **Time:** Hours saved, cycle time reduced, time to market
- **Percentages:** Growth rates, improvement %, efficiency gains
- **Volume:** Customers, projects, team size, transactions
- **Quality:** Satisfaction scores, error rates, accuracy, SLA
